## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an atmosphere of grounded safety, warmth, and unshakeable reassurance for women navigating urban spaces across Bengaluru. Drawing inspiration from the deep canopy of Cubbon Park, old rain trees, and sunlit terracotta avenues, the interface eliminates cold institutional anxiety. It replaces transactional fear with an inviting, protective harbor.

The design movement combines **Warm Organic Minimalism** with **Tactile Clarity**:
- **Zero-Friction Reassurance:** Core actions are instant, high-contrast, and legible even in transit, low-light autos, or high-stress situations.
- **Garden City Warmth:** A palette rooted in emerald forest depth, Gulmohar bloom accents, and soft jasmine-cream surfaces creates a welcoming, calm environment instead of a sterile panic dashboard.
- **Gentle Human Presence:** Soft pill radii, approachable typography, and human-scale micro-copy avoid militaristic language, opting for clear, dignified, and empowering protective mechanisms.

## Colors

The palette balances profound visual groundedness with decisive emergency contrast, honoring Bengaluru's natural and architectural textures:

- **Primary (`#1B4D3E` — Forest Canopy):** A deep, reassuring green reminiscent of Bangalore's heritage rain trees. Used for primary safety CTAs, key navigation anchors, active state indicators, and authoritative confirmations.
- **Secondary (`#E06D53` — Gulmohar Bloom):** A balanced terracotta-coral that provides energetic contrast without inducing blind panic. Reserved for high-priority emergency actions, SOS beacons, and live route deviation warnings.
- **Tertiary (`#D99B26` — Sampige Gold):** An amber-yellow accent used for active status indicators, live battery alerts, verified community safety nodes, and ongoing check-ins.
- **Neutral Surface Palette:**
  - Base Background: `#F7F5EE` (Warm Jasmine Cream) provides a gentle, non-glaring canvas that performs well in dark auto rides and direct afternoon sun.
  - Surface Raised: `#FFFFFF` (Crisp Milk) offers clear separation for cards and actionable modules.
  - Text & Outlines: `#12241D` (Deep Night Pine) delivers crisp 13:1+ contrast on cream backgrounds, ensuring effortless readability at a glance.
  - Muted Borders: `#E4E0D2` (Sun-dried Clay) creates soft containment without adding visual noise.

## Typography

The type system blends the approachable, geometric curves of **Plus Jakarta Sans** with the utilitarian precision of **Inter**:

- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Headlines, Display & Labels):** Provides warmth, reassurance, and modern character. Its open apertures remain legible when users glance at their phones while walking through poorly lit streets or bustling metro stations.
- **Inter (Body Copy, Route Information & Disclaimers):** Delivers clean optical rhythm, ensuring numbers, location names, contact details, and emergency instructions are unmistakably clear.
- **Hierarchy Rules:**
  - Emergency headings prioritize bold weight with restrained tracking to prevent visual crowding.
  - Body copy maintains generous line heights to preserve calm scanning speeds during tense moments.
  - Small labels utilize slightly increased letter tracking for immediate recognition on compact mobile screens.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an accessible, touch-first fluid grid optimized for thumb-reach ergonomics:

- **Mobile Viewport Focus (Primary):** Utilizes a 4-column fluid grid with 16px (`1rem`) outer margins and 12px gutters. Primary safety actions and the panic anchor are anchored in the lower 40% of the screen for one-handed thumb activation.
- **Tablet & Desktop Reflow:** Scales to 8 columns (tablet) and 12 columns (desktop) with 24px margins, maintaining a maximum centered content width of 640px for safety-critical dashboards to prevent eye strain and fragmentation.
- **Rhythm & Touch Targets:**
  - Minimum tap target size is strictly 48x48px, expanding to a minimum of 64px height for SOS and emergency actions.
  - Layout density remains low and airy; critical status elements must never be packed side-by-side without at least `space-md` (`1rem`) clearance.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects deep, muddy drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Layering** and **Soft Botanical Ambient Glows**:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - **Tier 0 (Canvas):** `#F7F5EE` (Jasmine Cream) base layer.
  - **Tier 1 (Cards & Modules):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface lifted by a soft 1px border of `#E4E0D2` and a subtle ambient shadow: `0 4px 16px rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.05)`.
  - **Tier 2 (Floating Action Trays & Bottom Sheets):** `#FFFFFF` with `0 8px 30px rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.09)` and a top border highlight of `#F7F5EE`.
  - **Tier 3 (Urgent Modals & Emergency SOS Anchor):** Deep interactive elevation with an ambient ring: `0 12px 36px rgba(224, 109, 83, 0.22)`.
- **Sensory Boundaries:** High contrast is maintained through subtle, warm borders rather than heavy drop shadows, keeping the UI light, uncluttered, and battery-efficient.

## Shapes

With a roundedness factor of `2`, the shape vocabulary balances structural reliability with protective softness:

- **Base Radius (`rounded-md` / 8px):** Applied to form fields, nested tags, and compact alerts.
- **Intermediate Radius (`rounded-lg` / 16px):** Standard across cards, location panels, contact badges, and modal dialogs.
- **Expanded Radius (`rounded-xl` / 24px):** Utilized for bottom sheets, quick-action tiles, and prominent status banners.
- **Full Pill (`rounded-full`):** Reserved for primary interactive controls, toggle filters, floating contact chips, and the central SOS trigger. The continuous curve conveys comfort and removes harsh edges.

## Components

### Buttons & SOS Controls
- **Primary Safety Action:** Pill-shaped, solid `#1B4D3E` with `#FFFFFF` label, min-height 52px, active state darkens to `#13382D`.
- **Emergency SOS Trigger:** Distinctive dual-ring pill or circular button. Filled with `#E06D53` (Gulmohar Bloom), featuring a subtle pulsating ambient ring of `rgba(224, 109, 83, 0.25)`. Includes an intentional 1.5-second hold-to-activate mechanism to prevent false triggers while ensuring immediate reach.
- **Secondary / Ghost Buttons:** Outlined with a 1.5px border of `#1B4D3E`, transparent background, and `#1B4D3E` text.

### Cards & Safe Haven Badges
- **Status Cards:** Solid white background, 16px radius, bordered by `#E4E0D2`. Incorporates an emerald left-edge accent (4px width) when monitoring or active protection is running smoothly.
- **Safe Haven Module:** Highlights verified community spots (Namma Metro stations, 24/7 pharmacies, police kiosks) with a gentle `#EDF3EE` surface, `#1B4D3E` icons, and a bold distance label.

### Form Inputs & Emergency Contacts
- **Input Fields:** Soft cream fill (`#FFFFFF`), 12px radius, `#12241D` text, 48px touch height. Focus states transition the border smoothly to `#1B4D3E` with a 2px outer aura.
- **Trusted Circle Contact Item:** Flat list card with quick-call and live-share toggles. Features user initials inside an earthy terracotta or sage circular avatar.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **Navigation Chips:** 36px height, rounded-full shape. Unselected chips have a `#FFFFFF` fill with an `#E4E0D2` stroke; selected chips switch to `#1B4D3E` fill with pure white text and an integrated checkmark icon.

### Live Route Deviation Banner
- A sticky persistent bar spanning screen width with 16px rounded bottom corners. Uses a warm tertiary background (`#FFF8EC`) with `#D99B26` and `#12241D` typography for reassuring, real-time trip verification.
